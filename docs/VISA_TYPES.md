# Visa Types and Tax Implications

## Supported Visa Types

Taxzy.ai provides specialized tax guidance for the following visa categories:

### F-1 Student Visa

**Tax Residency**: Generally considered nonresident aliens for the first 5 calendar years

**Key Considerations**:
- Exempt individual status
- Form 8843 requirements
- Scholarship and fellowship taxation
- On-campus vs. off-campus employment
- Practical Training (OPT/CPT) implications

**Common Forms**:
- Form 1040-NR
- Form 8843
- Form W-4 (special instructions)

### J-1 Exchange Visitor

**Tax Residency**: Generally nonresident for first 2 years (students/trainees) or exempt for 2 out of 6 years (professors/researchers)

**Key Considerations**:
- Two-year home residency requirement
- Substantial presence test exceptions
- Fellowship and scholarship taxation
- Employment restrictions

**Common Forms**:
- Form 1040-NR
- Form 8843
- State tax returns

### H-1B Specialty Occupation

**Tax Residency**: Subject to substantial presence test

**Key Considerations**:
- Resident alien status (typically after 183 days)
- Worldwide income taxation
- Tax treaty benefits
- Social Security and Medicare taxation
- State tax implications

**Common Forms**:
- Form 1040 (if resident)
- Form 1040-NR (if nonresident)
- State tax returns

### OPT (Optional Practical Training)

**Tax Residency**: Depends on duration in US

**Key Considerations**:
- Extension of F-1 status
- Social Security/Medicare tax exemptions (first 5 years)
- STEM OPT extension implications
- Unemployment periods
- Employment verification

**Common Forms**:
- Form 1040-NR (typically)
- Form 8843
- State tax returns

### CPT (Curricular Practical Training)

**Tax Residency**: Same as F-1 student status

**Key Considerations**:
- Part-time vs. full-time CPT
- Impact on OPT eligibility
- Tax withholding requirements
- Social Security/Medicare exemptions

**Common Forms**:
- Form 1040-NR
- Form 8843
- W-2 from employer

## California-Specific Considerations

### State Tax Residency
- Different from federal tax residency
- Based on domicile and time in California
- Part-year resident rules
- Nonresident taxation of California-source income

### Key California Forms
- Form 540 (Resident)
- Form 540-NR (Nonresident/Part-year)
- Schedule CA (Adjustments)

### Special Considerations
- California does not conform to all federal tax provisions
- State SDI (State Disability Insurance)
- Mental Health Services Tax
- Different standard deductions

## Tax Treaties

The United States has tax treaties with many countries that may provide:
- Reduced withholding rates
- Exemptions for certain types of income
- Special provisions for students and trainees

**Common Treaty Benefits**:
- Exemption of scholarship/fellowship income
- Personal exemption amounts
- Reduced tax rates on certain income
- Teaching and research exemptions

## Important Deadlines

- **April 15**: Federal and California tax filing deadline
- **June 15**: Extended deadline with proper extension
- **Throughout year**: Quarterly estimated tax payments (if applicable)
